Building a website can sound complicated. You might think you need to understand hosting, plugins, themes, databases and lots of technical settings.

NotionPress takes a different approach.

## Why NotionPress?

NotionPress lets you use Notion as your content management system. If you already know how to create a page in Notion, you already understand most of what you need to write for your website.

You write your articles in Notion, organise them with simple fields, and NotionPress turns them into a proper website.

There is no separate website editor to learn. You do not need to log into a complicated dashboard every time you want to publish something.

## Why Notion alone?

Notion is great for writing and organising information, but it is not designed to be a full public website.

NotionPress gives you the simplicity of Notion while adding the things you expect from a website, including proper pages, SEO-friendly URLs, search-engine metadata, RSS, sitemaps and a fast public website.

## Is NotionPress better than WordPress?

For everyone? No.

WordPress is extremely powerful and has thousands of plugins, themes and integrations. It is a better choice if you need a highly customised website, an online store or complex business features.

But that flexibility also makes WordPress more complicated.

For someone who simply wants to publish articles, guides, personal notes, a portfolio or a small community website, NotionPress can be much easier to manage.

Instead of learning how to manage a website, you can focus on what actually matters: creating useful content.

## Who is NotionPress for?

NotionPress is built for people who want their own website without becoming a website expert.

It is a good fit for bloggers, writers, creators, students, small communities, hobbyists and anyone who wants a simple place to publish online.

Write in Notion. Let NotionPress handle the website.
